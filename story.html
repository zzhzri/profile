02 / My Story

How I got here

Not a straight road, but every turn taught me something.

  1. Growing up

    Born and raised in Kota Kinabalu

    I grew up in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah and went to secondary school at SMK Kinarut in Papar. I sat for my SPM in 2023.

  2. 2024

    A fourth choice that grew on me

    To be honest, Information Management was my fourth choice in UPU. I accepted it because I wanted to keep studying and started my diploma at UiTM Kampus Samarahan 1 in Sarawak in September 2024. It turned out to be more interesting than I expected especially the parts about how information is organised, described and kept safe. I also met amazing people along the way.

  3. Along the way

    Discovering code

    Through my database classes I started building web systems with PHP and MySQL. Seeing something I typed turn into a working system was what hooked me and now I build things even when they are not part of an assignment.

  4. Jul – Sep 2026

    Internship at SESB

    I did my industrial training in the Security & Intelligence Department at Sabah Electricity Sdn. Bhd. I saw that staff still applied for access cards on paper then I suggested putting it online and built the system myself. I also built a system for reporting faulty CCTV cameras and it was the first time my work was used to solve a real problem.

  5. What's next

    Work experience then a degree

    My plan is to finish my diploma in March 2027 then get some work experience and then continue to a degree in September 2027. Some of my achievements might look small to others but each one means a lot to me.
